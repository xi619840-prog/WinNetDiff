# Community conduct

Be respectful, specific, and patient. People reporting network failures may have limited connectivity or little Windows experience. Critique the evidence and implementation without personal attacks. Harassment, discriminatory remarks, threats, doxxing, and sharing another person's private logs or credentials are not acceptable.

Maintainers may edit or remove privacy-sensitive content, close abusive discussions, and limit participation when needed. Explain moderation decisions without republishing the sensitive material. For a conduct concern, contact Alden through the repository's available private contact mechanism after the public repository is established; no private email address is published in this project. If no private channel is available, open a minimal request for private contact without naming affected individuals or disclosing the incident publicly.

Good-faith technical disagreement and responsible security reports are welcome. Security issues should follow [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).
